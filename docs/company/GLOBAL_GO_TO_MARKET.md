# Chasum — Global Go-To-Market Strategy

**Status:** Durable company strategy — implementation deferred until governed roadmap gates  
**Parent:** [Chasum Bible](./CHASUM_BIBLE.md)  
**Roadmap:** [Master Roadmap](./MASTER_ROADMAP.md)

This document preserves how Chasum should become discoverable, evaluable and adoptable around the world without allowing marketing claims to outrun product truth.

It is **not** a second roadmap and **not** a second country-readiness source of truth.

## Durable principle

**GLOBAL PRODUCT READINESS + GLOBAL DISCOVERY + DELIBERATE REGIONAL ACTIVATION.**

Global Readiness answers:

> Can Chasum operate correctly and safely in this market?

Global Go-To-Market answers:

> How does a service business in this market discover, evaluate, switch to and recommend Chasum?

Chasum needs both.

## Truth before reach

Chasum may become discoverable in a country before that country is commercially activated.

**MARKETING MUST NEVER OUTRUN PRODUCT TRUTH.**

Public country/region surfaces must derive their availability state from the governed Global Readiness product truth and distinguish states such as:

- commercially available;
- Private Alpha / design partner;
- waitlist / coming later;
- unsupported.

Do not advertise country-specific payments, tax handling, invoicing/legal behavior, communications, compliance, language support, or other regional capabilities unless they are actually supported.

Marketing must not maintain an independent country-capability list.

## Global growth engine

Preserve this long-term acquisition flywheel:

**Google / AI discovery  
→ Chasum country / industry / comparison content  
→ signup / demo / assisted switching  
→ governed Chasum migration  
→ successful operating business  
→ customer interaction / Powered by Chasum  
→ reviews / referrals / partners  
→ additional businesses discover Chasum.**

Traffic is useful only when it advances a business toward successful activation.

## Durable discovery architecture

Design toward reusable, high-value public content families such as:

- `/industries/{industry}`
- `/countries/{country}`
- `/compare/{competitor}`
- `/partners`

These are future content families, not authorization to generate them now.

Do **not** mass-generate thin SEO pages. Country, industry and comparison pages must contain useful, truthful market/product information and reflect actual readiness.

Discovery must support both:

- traditional search / SEO;
- AI-answer discovery / AEO / GEO.

Content should make Chasum understandable to both people and answer engines through clear product facts, structured information, authoritative first-party explanations and honest availability states.

## Long-term distribution channels

Preserve these future channels:

1. Search / SEO.
2. AI assistant / answer-engine discovery.
3. Industry-specific content.
4. Country / regional content.
5. Competitor-alternative / switching content.
6. Assisted migration.
7. Powered by Chasum organic exposure.
8. Customer referrals.
9. Chasum Partner Program.
10. Agencies, consultants, accountants/bookkeepers and technology partners.
11. Review/discovery platforms such as G2, Capterra and GetApp where commercially appropriate.
12. Integration ecosystems.
13. Social, video and educational content.
14. Regional paid acquisition only after product-market evidence exists.

Do not treat all channels as simultaneous launch requirements.

## Market Readiness Matrix

The **Global Readiness Foundation** owns or prepares the single governed Market Readiness Matrix.

Where relevant, it should express:

- country / region;
- supported languages;
- locale;
- currency;
- timezone / DST;
- tax readiness;
- invoice / legal readiness;
- international address / phone readiness;
- payment providers;
- email / SMS / voice providers;
- consent / privacy / compliance;
- product readiness;
- migration readiness;
- support readiness;
- marketing readiness;
- activation status.

Marketing activation consumes this product truth. It does not duplicate or override it.

Future implementation should expose a clean machine-readable readiness source so website content, onboarding, sales/support guidance and Summer can remain aligned.

## Deliberate regional activation

Do not permanently lock a worldwide launch sequence before Global Readiness research supports it.

Preserve the strategic concept of phased activation:

1. Canada / United States foundation.
2. Additional English-language markets.
3. Selected Caribbean design-partner markets.
4. Broader language/localization regions after the underlying product foundation is ready.

**Trinidad & Tobago and Guyana remain explicit candidate early international design-partner markets.**

Their value is strategic: founder/family market familiarity may help Chasum validate real international operating assumptions with trusted design partners.

**Jamaica and Barbados** may also be evaluated as Caribbean candidates, subject to readiness and research.

This is **not** authorization to launch or market commercial availability in any of those countries.

Exact activation order remains a future Product Owner decision informed by the Market Readiness Matrix, product-market evidence, provider capability, support capacity and commercial opportunity.

## Partner strategy

Preserve a future **Chasum Partner Program** concept.

Potential partner types include:

- web / marketing agencies;
- accountants / bookkeepers;
- business consultants;
- industry consultants;
- payment / POS specialists;
- technology providers.

Partners may eventually contribute to acquisition, onboarding and migration.

Referral economics, certification, permissions, support obligations and commercial terms remain future governed work.

## Viral and referral distribution

Preserve future consideration of:

- **Powered by Chasum** on eligible/free public booking experiences;
- customer referral programs;
- business-to-business referrals;
- authentic reviews;
- verified case studies.

Branding/removal behavior must remain consistent with Chasum plan entitlements and approved pricing/product policy.

Do not manufacture reviews, endorsements, customer outcomes or geographic traction.

## Switching is acquisition

Issue #73 migration/import capability is not only operational infrastructure. It is also a future acquisition advantage.

The intended promise is:

> **Switch to Chasum without rebuilding your business.**

That promise may be marketed only for the entities, workflows and source conditions that are genuinely Production accepted.

Competitor comparison content must remain factual, current and non-deceptive. Do not claim parity, superiority or supported migration breadth that has not been demonstrated.

## Measurement

Future Global GTM measurement should connect:

**discovery  
→ visit  
→ signup / demo  
→ onboarding  
→ migration  
→ activation  
→ first successful operation / booking  
→ paid conversion  
→ retention  
→ referral.**

Do not optimize vanity traffic independently of successful business activation.

Country, channel and content reporting should ultimately help answer:

- Which businesses discover Chasum?
- Which are qualified for the markets Chasum can actually support?
- Which complete onboarding and migration?
- Which become operational?
- Which retain and refer others?

## Relationship to Summer

Summer should eventually understand the business's explicit market context and Chasum's governed readiness truth so it can:

- explain whether a capability is available in a market;
- guide onboarding and switching accurately;
- avoid promising unsupported regional behavior;
- surface appropriate support or waitlist paths;
- learn from activation outcomes without inventing market facts.

Summer must not become a parallel source of country availability truth.

## Governance

1. **Global Readiness owns market capability truth.**
2. **Global GTM consumes that truth.**
3. Country marketing does not self-authorize product availability.
4. New country activation remains Product Owner-governed.
5. Marketing claims must match Production-accepted capability.
6. Country/industry/comparison content must be substantive; no thin-page generation program.
7. Paid acquisition follows evidence, not aspiration.
8. Issue #73 switching claims wait for the corresponding migration capability to be Production accepted.
9. The current roadmap sequence remains unchanged.
10. This document preserves strategy only; it does **not** authorize implementation now.

## Roadmap placement

Current governed sequence remains:

1. Outside Private Alpha readiness / Issue #73.
2. Global Readiness Foundation.
3. Commercial SaaS Gate B.
4. Summer Business Manager horizontal v1.
5. Native material implementation.
6. Broader regional / public expansion.

The Global Readiness Foundation should prepare the Market Readiness Matrix and extension points. Broader regional/public expansion later activates markets and GTM channels deliberately.
